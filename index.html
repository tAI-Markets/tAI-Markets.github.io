How it works

A clearing house for inference

You see a card payment, a balance, and a key. Underneath, providers collateralize, credits settle on Tempo, and every redemption clears on-chain.

01 / BUY

Card → credits

Stripe checkout credits your account. 1 USD = 1 USD of credits on a tk_ key, issued in the same request.

02 / PRICE

Index-set, on-chain

Credit prices mirror the live open inference market, published on-chain next to OpenRouter spot at the desk.

03 / SPEND

Point your harness

OpenAI- and Anthropic-compatible at api.tai.markets. An empty balance returns HTTP 402 with a top-up link.

04 / HEDGE

Lock the price

Lock today's index for 30 or 90 days. Same card, same credits, dated redemption.

Properties

Why credits hold

Backed 150%

Providers lock stablecoins above every credit issued, and the floor is enforced on-chain. Redemption stays guaranteed.

One credit, any provider

Inference is standardized across providers: your credit redeems whoever serves the compute, at the same index.

Provider of last resort

If no provider can serve a redemption, the house buys replacement inference. Settlements stay whole.

Priced by the market

The index mirrors live compute costs on-chain, so credits track what inference is actually worth.
